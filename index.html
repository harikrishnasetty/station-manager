Your job: keep the station flowing.
1. Trains wait in the blue bays at each end.
2. Click a waiting train (or its label) to select it.
3. Click a free platform to send it in.
4. Turn the W or E signal green to let it in (match its direction).
5. When it says READY, open the OUT-E (right end) or OUT-W (left end) signal on that platform to release it the way it will leave.
6. Serve trains, avoid 5 crashes. SPACE pause · R restart.
Signals are the round lights above the track. Green = proceed, Red = stop. Only one train may use a station throat at a time — others wait in their bay until it clears.
