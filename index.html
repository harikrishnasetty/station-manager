How to play

Local trains need two green lights: the main W/E and their platform signal. They dock, then you open the platform's other-end signal to send them out.

Express & Goods skip platforms — just the main signal and they use the green bypass. Big bonus.

Each light turns red automatically once a train claims it. Only one train uses a throat at a time.
