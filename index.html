Your job: use the signals to route trains.
1. Trains wait in the blue bays at each end.
2. Open the W (left) or E (right) main signal for that side.
3. Open an IN1W…IN4W (left platform end) or IN1E…IN4E (right platform end) signal — a waiting train automatically goes to that free platform.
4. When it says READY, open that platform's OUT-E (right end) or OUT-W (left end) signal and the train departs automatically.
5. Serve trains, avoid 5 crashes. SPACE pause · R restart.
Signals are the round lights above the track. Green = proceed, Red = stop. Only one train may use a station throat at a time — others wait in their bay until it clears.
