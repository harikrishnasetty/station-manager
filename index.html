Your job: use the signals to route trains.
1. Trains wait in the blue bays at each end.
2. Open the W (left) or E (right) main signal for that side.
3. Open that platform's signal on the same side (P1L…P4L left end or P1R…P4R right end) — the train automatically routes to that free platform. Entry, exit and main signals all turn red once it enters.
4. When it says READY, open the platform signal on the side it will leave — the train departs automatically and that side's main signal turns red.
5. EXPRESS and GOODS trains skip the platforms: just open the main W/E signal and they use the green bypass line straight through (big bonus).
6. Serve trains, avoid 5 crashes. SPACE pause · R restart.
Signals are the round lights above the track. Green = proceed, Red = stop. Only one train may use a station throat at a time — others wait in their bay until it clears.
